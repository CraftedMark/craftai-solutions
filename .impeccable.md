# CraftAI Solutions — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Small business owners and enterprise decision-makers (IT directors, CTOs, ops managers) looking for:
1. **UniFi network integrations** — professional setup, configuration, and ongoing management
2. **AI workflow automation** — integrating AI into existing business processes to save time and reduce friction

They arrive via referral or search, likely on desktop during work hours. They need to quickly understand what CraftAI does, see proof of competence, and have a clear path to get in touch. Trust is the #1 emotion — they're handing over infrastructure and business processes.

### Brand Personality
**Professional. Innovative. Approachable.**

- Professional: This isn't a side hustle. CraftAI delivers enterprise-grade work.
- Innovative: AI-forward, modern tooling, ahead of the curve.
- Approachable: Not cold or corporate. Mark is hands-on, personal, direct.

Voice: Confident but not arrogant. Technical but not jargon-heavy. Direct.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Modern tech meets human warmth — clean lines, generous whitespace, subtle depth
- **Palette:**
  - Primary: Deep slate (#0F172A) — authority, trust
  - Accent: Electric blue (#3B82F6) — innovation, technology
  - Secondary accent: Amber (#F59E0B) — warmth, approachability, energy
  - Surface light: (#F8FAFC) — clean, open
  - Surface dark: (#1E293B) — professional dark mode
  - Text light: (#F1F5F9)
  - Text dark: (#334155)
- **Typography:**
  - Display: Space Grotesk (tight tracking, bold weights) — modern, geometric, technical
  - Body: DM Sans (regular weight) — warm, readable, approachable
- **Theme:** Both light and dark mode (system preference with manual toggle)
- **Anti-references:** Cluttered MSP/IT company sites with stock photos of servers. Generic WordPress templates. Overly corporate "synergy" language.
- **References (aspirational):** Linear.app (clean, dark, purposeful), Vercel.com (bold typography, clear value prop), Tailwind CSS site (developer-friendly but beautiful)

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over cleverness** — Every section answers one question. No ambiguity about what CraftAI does.
2. **Show, don't tell** — Use diagrams, icons, and visual hierarchy instead of walls of text.
3. **Trust through craft** — Pixel-perfect execution signals competence. If the site looks good, the work will be good.
4. **Dark mode is first-class** — Not an afterthought. Both themes should feel intentional.
5. **Speed to contact** — Every page has a clear CTA. Reduce friction to "let's talk."
